# Kamunity Platform: Complete Site Map & User Brief

## Executive Summary

Kamunity is a comprehensive community engagement platform that transforms how people discover, connect, and create meaningful change in their communities. The platform follows a progressive engagement model: **News → Chat → Rooms → Clubs → Communities → Values Exchange**, guiding users from passive consumption to active community leadership.

---

## Complete Site Map & Features

### 🏠 **Homepage** (`/`)
**Purpose**: Welcome & orientation hub with clear user journey
**Features**:
- Hero section with Kai (AI community guide) introduction
- Launch funnel showing 3-step progression (Discover → Connect → Create)
- Community news preview with audio player
- Upcoming events countdown
- Community beta phase information
- Direct navigation to News and Room Generation

**User Journey**: Entry point → News browsing OR Room creation

---

### 📰 **News Hub** (`/news`)
**Purpose**: Community story discovery and engagement
**Features**:
- Curated news from golden threads (successful community initiatives)
- AI-powered summary panel with Kai insights
- Filter by timeframe (today, week, month) and perspectives
- Threaded comments system with promotion mechanics
- Content types: Kamunity stories, room summaries, chat highlights, external stories
- Real-time engagement tracking and analytics

**User Journey**: News → Related Chat → Room Creation

---

### 💬 **Chat System**
#### Chat Hub (`/chat`)
**Purpose**: Browse active community conversations
**Features**:
- Live chat discovery interface
- Filter by topic, activity level, and community
- Real-time participant counts and engagement metrics

#### Individual Chats (`/chat/[id]`)
**Purpose**: Real-time community discussions
**Features**:
- Live messaging with WebSocket connectivity
- Kai AI moderation and guidance
- Quick polls and community tools
- Reaction system and engagement tracking
- Thread progression to room creation
- Enhanced chat headers with context

**User Journey**: Chat participation → Room formation → Club membership

---

### 🎯 **Rooms System**
#### Rooms Hub (`/rooms`)
**Purpose**: Discover and join action-oriented community groups
**Features**:
- Grid of active focus rooms
- Filter by category, impact score, and member count
- Room stats and objectives preview
- Quick action buttons for immediate engagement

#### Room Generator (`/rooms/generate`)
**Purpose**: Create new community action groups
**Features**:
- 3 generator types: Fast Track (60-70%), Balanced (75-85%), Comprehensive (90-100%)
- Quick actions selection (3-5 actions minimum)
- Progressive form completion
- Spec sheet generation with "day in the life" stories
- Integration with golden threads data

#### Individual Rooms (`/rooms/[id]`)
**Purpose**: Collaborative community action spaces
**Features**:
- Objective tracking and progress metrics
- Member directory and role assignments
- Resource sharing and task management
- Impact logging and measurement tools
- Quick actions for immediate engagement
- Integration with chat system

#### Focus Rooms (`/rooms/[id]/focus`)
**Purpose**: Immersive room experience with live simulations
**Features**:
- Live chat simulations
- Activity feed with real-time updates
- Progress metrics and impact visualization
- Member engagement tracking
- Enhancement options for incomplete rooms

#### Room Enhancement (`/rooms/[id]/enhance`)
**Purpose**: Improve room completeness and effectiveness
**Features**:
- Completeness analysis (required vs optional fields)
- Enhancement suggestions and guided improvements
- Progressive disclosure of advanced features

## Platform Architecture & User Flow

### Core Progression Path
```
Homepage → News Discovery → Chat Participation → Room Creation → Club Formation → Community Federation → Values Exchange
```

### Alternative Entry Points
- **Direct Room Creation**: Users with specific goals can start with room generation
- **Community Browsing**: Explore existing clubs and communities before engaging
- **Event Participation**: Join through specific community events and initiatives

### Retention Mechanisms
- **Progressive Disclosure**: Features unlock as users advance through engagement levels
- **Social Recognition**: Badges, impact scores, and community acknowledgment
- **Real Results**: Tangible outcomes from community efforts
- **Peer Connections**: Lasting relationships formed through shared action
